HOW TO PLAY

Type the letters to blast warheads. Blasts chain. Protect your cities.
All the rulesWarheads fall toward your cities. Type a warhead’s letters to detonate a blast on it. Any warhead caught in a blast explodes too, and each one in the chain is worth more than the last. Let them bunch up, then pop the middle.
Left-hand keys defend the left sky, right-hand keys the right. Each hand locks its own target.
Mistake = your hand jams for 0.7s (that sky is defenceless) and your combo breaks.
Alternating hands and a high combo make blasts bigger.
Gold LINK pair: finish one, then the twin on the other screen within 2.6s for a giant RESONANCE blast.
MIRV splits into more warheads mid-fall. DART is a fast single letter.
Clearing a wave pays a bonus per surviving city, and every second wave rebuilds one. Lose all eight and it’s over. Cluster bonus: blasting a bunch multiplies chain points, and squads of three converge on purpose, so waiting can pay. Chains of 5+ send a shockwave to the other screen.
Backspace/Esc drops your locks.
